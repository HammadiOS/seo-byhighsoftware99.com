    Local SEO Methodology: Prominence, Proximity & Category Precision

    Google's Local Pack and Google Maps rankings revolve around three core ranking groups: Relevance (primary and secondary GBP categories, service descriptions, and localized landing page copy), Distance (verified physical address or service-area configuration), and Prominence (review velocity, keyword diversity in customer reviews, consistent citations, and local inbound links).

    High-Impact Local SEO Checklist

    • Select exact primary GBP category & 3–5 secondary categories: Primary category drives the majority of category-level Map Pack relevance.
    • Standardize NAP across Tier-1 aggregators first: Align Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Facebook, and Yelp to identical digit sequences and legal entity naming (remove trailing "LLC" mismatches across citations if not present on GBP).
    • Embed LocalBusiness JSON-LD & regional city proof on landing pages: Generate exact schema markup with our JSON-LD Schema Generator and explore our 39 regional pages in Service Locations.

    Local SEO FAQs

    Do minor abbreviations like St vs Street or Ste vs Suite hurt local SEO?

    Google understands standard USPS abbreviations, though exact consistency eliminates conflicting suite numbers, tracking phone discrepancies, and business name mismatches across aggregators.

    Why should you add UTM parameters to your Google Business Profile website URL?

    UTM parameters separate Map Pack sessions inside Google Analytics (GA4) from standard web search organic traffic so you can track conversions accurately.

    How do I create a direct Google Review link without third-party tools?

    Use https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID using the generator above—customers go straight to the 5-star review modal.
