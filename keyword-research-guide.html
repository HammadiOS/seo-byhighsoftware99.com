Keyword Research Process by HighSoftware99

HighSoftware99 structures keyword research into four discrete phases: Seed Query Extraction, Intent Classification, Entity Attribute Mapping, and Information Gain Evaluation. This methodology ensures every page addresses specific query intent with high Entity-Attribute-Value (EAV) density.

1. Seed Query Extraction

HighSoftware99 extracts root queries from industry search corpora, competitor ranking profiles, and user search logs. Target keywords are evaluated based on monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, and business intent metrics.

2. Search Intent Classification

Search engines classify intent into four primary categories: Informational, Navigational, Commercial, and Transactional. HighSoftware99 aligns page layouts and visual centerpiece components with the exact intent category detected in SERP features.

  • Informational: Requires detailed guides, structured lists, and clear entity definitions.
  • Commercial: Demands feature comparison tables and service benchmark grids.
  • Transactional: Utilizes audit inputs, call-to-action modules, and pricing breakdown elements.

3. Entity Attribute Mapping

Keywords reflect underlying real-world entities and their attributes. HighSoftware99 maps primary target keywords to secondary semantic nodes to form comprehensive topical clusters.
