Why On-Page Discipline Fixes Impressions Stuck on Page 6–7

When Google Search Console shows steady impressions around positions 60–75 with zero clicks, upgrading page-level intent clarity (tight front-loaded titles, clean direct answers, structured tables, and explicit internal links from topically relevant hubs) helps reclassify the URL toward page 1–2 long-tail queries where users click.

On-Page SEO Checklist FAQs

Which on-page SEO checks should happen before every URL goes live?

Verify title pixel width (≤580px), 130–155 char meta description, clean canonical tag, single H1, ordered headings, image dimensions/alt, contextual links, and valid JSON-LD.

Does keyword density percentages still matter in on-page SEO?

No—search models evaluate entity richness, direct question resolution, structured layout, and semantic clarity rather than repeating an exact keyword ratio.
